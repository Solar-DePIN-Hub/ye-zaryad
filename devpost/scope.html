Charge Ahead

A calm planner that tells you what will go dead in a blackout, and what to charge by when.

Decisions were relayed from Vadym Bilobrovets. This page is a visual companion to scope.md. He has not separately reviewed the English wording. Assumptions are marked.

Who does what

Now, later, cut

Now — the proof of concept
  • Paste outage text and tap hours on a 24-hour bar.
  • Phone, power bank, laptop, router, charging station, flashlight.
  • What dies and when. What to charge by when. A red warning that says what to charge first.
  • A calendar file. The page still opens offline after the first load.
  • English on screen. Sentences kept in one module.
Later
