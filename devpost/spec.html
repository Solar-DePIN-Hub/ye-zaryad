One journey through the pieces

Pieces, and the PRD section each one serves

Schedule parser and hour toggles — Schedule hours

Pure functions. Text or a tapped hour becomes merged ranges in minutes from midnight. Unreadable lines are returned beside the ranges, not thrown away. File: src/domain/parseSchedule.ts.

Kit editor — The energy kit

Presets are typical estimates. List order is who gets scarce energy first. File: src/components/KitEditor.tsx, numbers in src/domain/presets.ts.

Energy simulation — What will die and when

96 steps of 15 minutes. Charge on grid power, draw on outage, own battery then shared supply. This is the kernel and it is not faked. File: src/domain/simulate.ts.

Warning, day bar, battery chart — the day on screen

Rendered from the simulation result. The chart is inline SVG. Files under src/components/.

Action list and calendar file — The charge-by list

"Charge {name} by {time}" plus a CRLF .ics with one event and reminder per line. Built in the browser. File: src/domain/ics.ts.

Saved kit and offline shell

Key ye-zaryad:v1. The service worker is generated at build time by vite-plugin-pwa. English sentences live only in src/strings.ts.
