Є заряд — where things live

A page that turns outage hours and a short kit into two results: what runs out, and what to charge by when. This map is a snapshot of the finished code. It can drift if the files move.

Reference route only. It was not walked live with Vadym.

One action

Route

1. Read the hours — parseSchedule

src/domain/parseSchedule.ts, function parseSchedule. Text in, merged minute ranges out, plus any line that could not be read. Search for SAMPLE_SCHEDULE to see the demo day.

2. Walk 24 hours — simulate

src/domain/simulate.ts, function simulate. Ninety-six steps of 15 minutes. This is the part that decides a device has run out. Preset numbers are in src/domain/presets.ts.

3. Show the sentence — Warnings

src/components/Warnings.tsx, function Warnings. The red block is the sentence the tests look for. The charge lines are actionHeadline in src/domain/actions.ts, and the file is buildIcs in src/domain/ics.ts.

To change this, start here

  • Wording on screen: src/strings.ts only.
  • A preset wattage: src/domain/presets.ts.
  • What "runs out" means: simulate in src/domain/simulate.ts, then tests/simulate.test.ts.
  • Colours: the variables at the top of src/styles.css.

A practice to reuse

The product note says a red line should contain a time and the words that something runs out (devpost/prd.md, "What will die and when"). tests/e2e.spec.ts looks for that sentence, and tests/plan.test.ts checks the sample day both without and with the charging station. Writing the sentence you expect to see, then pointing a test at it, is the same move on another project: the check fails when the page stops saying the thing you promised.
